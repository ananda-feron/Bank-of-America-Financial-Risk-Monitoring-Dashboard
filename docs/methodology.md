# Metric definitions

All balance-sheet values are December 31 observations; income and loss flows span January 1–December 31. Amounts are USD dollars. Ratios and YoY changes are fractions.

| Calculated metric | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Common equity | Total equity − preferred equity | Common shareholders' book equity |
| Total provision | Funded provision + unfunded commitment provision | Negative is a reserve release |
| Total credit-loss allowance | Funded allowance + unfunded commitment allowance | Broader than the loan-only reserve |
| Loan/deposit | Gross loans / deposits | Funding mix indicator |
| Cash/assets | Issuer cash and equivalents / assets | Broad cash share, not LCR |
| Equity/assets | Total equity / assets | Book capitalization, not regulatory capital |
| Net charge-off ratio | Annual net charge-offs / mean(prior/current gross loans) | Approximate average balance denominator |
| Allowance/loans | Funded loan allowance / gross loans | Loan reserve coverage; excludes unfunded reserve from numerator |
| Trading exposure | Trading account assets / total assets | Limited market-risk proxy |
| Calculated ROA | Net income / mean(prior/current total assets) | Two-point average approximation |
| Calculated ROE | Net income / mean(prior/current total equity) | Total-equity return, not return on common equity |
| Calculated efficiency | Noninterest expense / GAAP revenue net of interest expense | No FTE adjustment |
| YoY | (Current − prior) / prior | Missing if prior <= 0 or unavailable |

Zero denominators never generate zero ratios. Missing components propagate through sums and differences; no partial total is published. Negative provision and negative net income are legitimate and are preserved. Growth can exceed 100% or fall below −100%, so range tests do not impose a blanket 0–100% rule.

2020 is an internal support year, excluded from the annual dashboard but retained in provenance. The current snapshot/mapping has no 2020 gross-loan balance. It does have 2020 assets and total equity, permitting the approximate 2021 ROA/ROE. A two-point mean does not reproduce the bank's daily or monthly average balances.

Selection is latest filing within a reviewed tag priority, as of a configurable cutoff. Different metrics may therefore have different source filing years. Do not present this series as information available to an investor at each historical year-end. Use accession/date lineage to review restatements.
